# Kala game surface

Mode: Operate

## Direction

Mintfield Broadcast. The game remains immediately recognizable and easy to operate; the palette supplies the identity, information density, typography roles, and a traced safe-zone boundary during volley transitions.

## World

- Deep Space Blue `#0d2d46` anchors the shell and arena ground.
- Frosted Mint `#e6fbda` and Lemon Chiffon `#fff8d3` provide the working surfaces and foreground type.
- Moss Green `#85d176` marks the active safe boundary, ready states, and confirmed actions.
- Dusk Blue `#3f5ba4` carries secondary information and team distinction.
- Warm coral is reserved for Red team and critical hit states because those are explicit game semantics.
- The visual tone is fresh and light, like a field guide, with calm, precise controls.
- Barlow Condensed is display type, Manrope is body/UI type, and DM Mono is reserved for phase labels, room codes, and measured values.

## First viewport

Keep the three-part desktop game shell: roster and match status, central arena, then loadout, weapons, room entry, and settings. Deep Space Blue remains the structural ground; the rails and bottom action surface become pale mint or lemon work surfaces with dark ink. On phones, a compact create/join strip comes first, then a dominant playable arena, horizontally scrollable combatant strip, and secondary loadout/settings. Keep reset and lock actions fixed within thumb reach above the phone safe area. TV mode expands the arena and hides player controls.

## Signature move

On every resolved volley, a lemon-colored marker traces the Moss Green safe-zone contour once, making the arena transition readable without hiding shots or player positions.

## User path and states

Each player sees only their own position marker while placing and aiming; opponents' coordinates and aim remain private. After all living players lock, reveal everyone's positions and shots together as the volley resolves. The TV view reveals no positions before that same reveal. Show waiting, locked, resolving, hit, safe, and finished states with clear text and palette-backed color. Room code, invite-link, and TV QR actions remain separate.

## Constraints and risk

Preserve game rules, text meaning, room controls, host actions, QR behavior, mobile controls, and TV privacy. Use Deep Space Blue to separate the mint and lemon work surfaces; reserve warm coral for Red-team and critical states. Avoid low-contrast Dusk Blue on dark backgrounds.
